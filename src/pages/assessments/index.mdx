Two individual assignments, 40% and 60%. Assignment 1 is a single-drone planner:
released week 3, due Friday of week 7. Assignment 2 extends it to five drones, two
charging pads and the shared corridor: released week 7, due Friday of week 12. Tutorials
are not graded; they are where both are built. Constraints are checked before performance
is compared, and a plan with a violation is not a valid plan.
