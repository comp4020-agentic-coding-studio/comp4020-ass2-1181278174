# Assessment

Tutorial attendance and lab participation contribute **20%**: all twelve weeks count,
with one attendance point and one participation point each week. The two individual
assignments contribute the remaining **80%**.

**Assignment 1 · 30%** builds a single-drone planner: released week 3, due Friday of
week 7. **Assignment 2 · 50%** extends it to five drones, two charging pads and the
shared corridor: released week 7, due Friday of week 12. Constraints are checked
before performance is compared, and a plan with a violation is not a valid plan.
