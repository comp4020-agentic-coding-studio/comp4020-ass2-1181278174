# Lectures

Twelve Monday lectures, one question each. The first three build a search you can
trust; week 4 makes it respect a battery; weeks 5 and 6 turn one route into a schedule;
weeks 7 and 8 divide the work between drones that share two charging pads; weeks 9 to 12
make five plans executable in one airspace. Week 9 carries the deck. Each page contains learning goals, a model boundary, a worked derivation or trace, a figure,
a checkpoint with an explanation, and a link to the matching tutorial. Read the lesson,
make a prediction, then use the tutorial to test it.
