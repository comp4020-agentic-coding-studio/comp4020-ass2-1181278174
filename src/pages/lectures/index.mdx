Twelve Monday lectures, one question each. The first three build a search you can
trust; week 4 makes it respect a battery; weeks 5 and 6 turn one route into a schedule;
weeks 7 and 8 divide the work between drones that share two charging pads; weeks 9 to 12
make five plans executable in one airspace. Week 9 carries the deck. Each page says what
the lecture covers, what you must be able to derive, and where it goes next.
