## Make the failure visible

Kofi works on simulation and testing in Slop University's Planning and Delivery Group.
He is interested in the evidence that makes an algorithm understandable: the route that
ran out of battery, the order that missed its deadline and the two reservations that
overlapped by one tick.

His tutorials begin with a prediction. Students change one thing, inspect the result and
explain whether it supports that prediction. Kofi helps turn unexpected behaviour into a
small, repeatable test, then connects that test back to the week's lecture.

## In this course

Kofi leads the Wednesday tutorials, records attendance and participation, and helps with
the workbench, strategy code and experiment records. You do not need a finished solution
to ask for help: bring the input you tried, the result you expected and what happened.

Questions about assessment decisions or extensions go to Ines. Questions about why your
drone is waiting at the kitchen are a good place to start with Kofi.
