me."

Dr Halvorsen-Tan wrote this course around one evening of deliveries because a semester on
one instance teaches more than a semester of examples. She gives the twelve lectures and
marks assignments 1 and 2 against the criteria on their pages.

Ask her about: what the course expects, why a rule is the way it is, marking, extensions.
