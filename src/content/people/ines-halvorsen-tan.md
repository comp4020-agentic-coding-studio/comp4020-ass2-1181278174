## Planning with limits

Ines is a researcher in Slop University's Planning and Delivery Group. Her work asks a
practical question: what should a search algorithm remember when the shortest route is
too expensive to fly? She studies energy-aware search and coordination between agents
that share a limited resource.

She built *Twenty Dinners, One Hill* around one evening of deliveries so students can
watch the same planner change over a semester. In her lectures, a counterexample comes
before a new technique: first find the assumption that broke, then decide what the
algorithm must do differently.

## In this course

Ines leads the Monday lectures, sets the assessment criteria and oversees marking.
She is the person to ask about the course's expectations, the reasons behind the model,
assessment feedback and extensions. Bring a small example when you want to discuss a
planning decision; it makes the assumptions easier to see.

For help running an experiment or debugging a strategy, start with Kofi in the tutorial.
