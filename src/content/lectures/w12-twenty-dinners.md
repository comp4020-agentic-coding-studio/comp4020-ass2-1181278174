## What the lecture covers

The path from spatial nodes to resource labels to space-time states, and the dependence
of ordering, assignment and coordination on each other. Three kinds of claim are kept
apart: properties that were proven, results that finite tests support, and
generalisations that nothing has verified. On small instances the exact optimum is
available; on twenty orders it is not, and the report says so.

## What you must be able to derive

Re-check the objective, the order denominator, task completeness and every constraint.
Report quality and compute cost on the twenty-order scenario without an optimality claim
you cannot support.
